FiveStarMoments Clipper

A private tool that sends FiveStarMoments' own short gaming clips to the owner's TikTok drafts.

Terms of Service

Last updated: 24 November 2026

FiveStarMoments Clipper is a personal tool used only by the owner of the FiveStarMoments accounts. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign up for it.

What the tool does

It uploads short video clips made by FiveStarMoments to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts. Nothing is posted automatically: the owner reviews each draft in the TikTok app and decides whether to post it.

Use of TikTok

The tool uses TikTok's official APIs and follows TikTok's Terms of Service and Developer Terms. The owner is responsible for the content they choose to post.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, for personal use, without any guarantee of availability.

Changes

These terms may be updated. The date above shows the latest version.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

FiveStarMoments Clipper is used only by its owner, so the only TikTok account it ever connects to is the owner's own.

Data the tool accesses

  • Basic profile info (display name and username), to confirm the right account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos to that account's drafts.

How it is stored

The TikTok login token is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never shared, sold, or sent anywhere except to TikTok when uploading.

What it does not do

  • It does not collect data about other TikTok users or viewers.
  • It does not use cookies, tracking, or advertising.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions.
